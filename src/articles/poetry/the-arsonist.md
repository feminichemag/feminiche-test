title: The Arsonist
author: Holly Norton
date: 2026-09-22T21:28:00.000-04:00
heroImage: /uploads/fire.jpg
excerpt: She learns / to cauterize her wounds, / keeps putting on / her asbestos suit.
poems:
  - text: |
      She strikes
      a match for any man
      who insinuates himself
      into her life,
      incinerates
      friends and lovers
      who leave,
      carries a torch
      for those who might,
      considers everything
      kindling.

      She packs up 
      the ashes
      in cardboard boxes,
      leaves a trail
      of charred remains,
      wipes them out
      of her mind.

      She learns
      to cauterize her wounds,
      keeps putting on 
      her asbestos suit.
authorBio: >
