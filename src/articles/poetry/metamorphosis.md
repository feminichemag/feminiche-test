title: Metamorphosis
author: Dariana Sârca
date: 2026-10-08T21:05:00.000-04:00
heroImage: /uploads/butterfly.jpg
heroCaption: '"The Butterfly," Winslow Homer, 1872. Oil on canvas. Public domain.'
poems:
  - text: |
      I wish I were a cocoon, 
      Just to know
      I can be something more.

      I wish I were the first to bloom, 
      Or the first not to bloom alone.

      I wish I were a cloud,
      If only 
      To transform.
      Or a withered leaf in the wind,
      Being carried by the storm.
authorBio: Dariana Sârca is a Master's student from Romania whose obsession with
  words has been shaping her entire life. She graduated with a degree in English
  and Latin, planning to pursue her passion further with a PhD in African
  American literature. Her interests also include poetry, theatre, and Hitchcock
  thrillers. Her poetry style could be described as New Romantic.
