title: Lingering in the mind

poems:
  - text: |+
      Though you have left my body
      you still linger in my mind
      casually painting images
      refusing to let me forget-

      and so your voice plays back
      the messages you ingrained so long ago
      stuck in my mind 
      wrapping my brain in soaked rags

      of doubts
      of insecurity
      the words lashing my self-esteem
      cutting new wounds upon my scars

      when you left my body
      I saw a glimmer of light
      until I realized you had wrapped 
      my mind in darkness
