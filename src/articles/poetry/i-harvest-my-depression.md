title: I Harvest My Depression
author: Leanne Kathleen Indigo
date: 2026-10-05T17:18:00.000-04:00
heroImage: /uploads/depression.jpg
poems:
  - text: |-
      I harvest my depression
      from a field lush with overgrown emotion
      attempt to process it into miniscule morsels
      easier to consume as it liquifies in my digestive tract
      my stomach aches for freedom
      as the despair refuses to pass
      perhaps if I drown it in drink
      the pain will become bearable
      at least my personality will become
      temporarily more bubbly
      an asterisked aside to an otherwise melancholy mood
      mist permeates the field
      as a scarecrow startles me
      from more pleasant emotions
      left un-plucked on the vines
      their fruit souring—
      at home, alone with my cat, my mind wanders
      from fruit to wine.
authorBio: Leanne Kathleen Indigo is a poet, speculative fiction and comic
  writer (L. K. Indigo), and singer (Elle Indigo) who draws inspiration from the
  mundane to the mystical, often speculating on love, life, and mental health.
  Her debut poetry book, Blank Canvas, is out now.
